## 3. Research context

The two case identifiers belong to the broader technical investigation documented throughout this repository, including CKKS behavior, TCC/Silent-Grant observations, account-bound persistence hypotheses, forensic reconstruction, and reproducibility work.

For evidentiary purposes, three layers should remain strictly separated:

1. **Documented fact:** the Apple Security case identifier and associated correspondence or submission record.
2. **Forensic observation:** artifacts, logs, databases, hashes, timestamps, binaries, and reproducible measurements contained in the evidence set.
3. **Research conclusion or hypothesis:** interpretation of those observations, including attack-path, causality, attribution, and architectural conclusions.

This separation is intentional. It preserves the distinction between primary evidence and interpretation and makes the repository more suitable for independent review.
